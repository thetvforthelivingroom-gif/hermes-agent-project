# US imposes fresh sanctions on Russia's VTB Bank over alleged Iran ties

On September 14, 2026 the United States imposed new sanctions on Russia's VTB Bank, accusing it of helping Iran evade Western sanctions. The Treasury Department noted that VTB had opened offices in Iran, established banking relationships with sanctioned Iranian lenders, and built financial channels to facilitate trade between Moscow and Tehran. The sanctions add to existing measures from 2022 and label VTB as "among the most comprehensively sanctioned financial institutions in the world," warning foreign banks that dealing with VTB could face secondary penalties.

Key points:
- Sanctions target VTB for facilitating Iran‑related transactions.
- Treasury cites office openings in Iran and correspondent banking with sanctioned Iranian entities.
- Foreign institutions risk secondary sanctions if they continue business with VTB.

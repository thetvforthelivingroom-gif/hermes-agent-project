# Trump says AI concerns are a ‘hoax’—calls for ‘very negative forces’ behind hype

President Donald Trump dismissed warnings about artificial‑intelligence safety as a “hoax” on September 13, 2026, posting on social media that concerns were being driven by “very negative forces” aiming to undermine U.S. competitiveness. In the same messages he claimed that a “sick conspiracy” was being promoted by political opponents and suggested that only a strong, smart president could keep AI development under control. The remarks came after CEOs of Anthropic, OpenAI and SpaceX called for a slowdown in “reckless” AI development, warning of potential existential risks. Trump’s statements were promptly criticized by tech leaders and analysts, who noted the disparity between his dismissive tone and the growing geopolitical and economic stakes surrounding AI.

Key points:
- Trump called AI safety fears a “hoax” and blamed political adversaries.
- He suggested a “strong and smart” president is the only safeguard.
- The comments followed an industry‑wide appeal for a slowdown in AI development.
- Critics labeled the remarks as a dismissal of legitimate risk concerns.

# Trump considers releasing more 9/11 records after families’ request

President Donald Trump said on September 13, 2026 that he would look into a request from families of the September 11 2001 victims to release additional records related to the attacks. In remarks made in Ireland before returning to the United States, Trump said, “I’m going to look at it when I get back,” indicating a willingness to review the families’ request but offering no timetable for any declassification.

Key points:
- Statement made on September 13, 2026 in Ireland.
- Trump did not commit to a specific action, only to consider the request.
- The request concerns possible disclosure of records that could illuminate alleged Saudi involvement.

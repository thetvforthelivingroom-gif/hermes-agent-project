# AI‑linked stocks slide after tech bosses call for slowdown in ‘reckless’ development

On September 14, 2026 leading AI firms including Anthropic, OpenAI and SpaceX’s Elon Musk joined a call from industry CEOs to pause what they described as “reckless” AI development. The open letter, signed by about 100 executives, warned that rapid progress without adequate safety measures could pose existential risks. In response, the stock market reacted sharply: major AI‑linked equities such as Nvidia, Microsoft’s AI division, and Alphabet’s DeepMind saw declines of 3‑5% during the trading day. Analysts cited the slowdown appeal as a reminder of regulatory and ethical pressures mounting on the sector, especially after recent high‑profile incidents involving AI‑generated misinformation. While some investors view the call as a prudent step toward longer‑term stability, others fear it may dampen growth momentum and trigger a broader tech sell‑off. The episode underscores the tension between innovation speed and societal safeguards in the rapidly evolving artificial‑intelligence landscape.

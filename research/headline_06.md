# EXCLUSIVE: From schoolyard sweets trader to Africa's richest man, Dangote eyes his biggest deal yet - Reuters

## Summary
Aliko Dangote, Nigeria’s billionaire and founder of the Dangote Group, launched Africa’s largest IPO to sell shares in his massive Lagos oil refinery. The offering of 4.1 billion ordinary shares at 525 naira each seeks to raise roughly 2.15 trillion naira (≈ $5.5 billion). If fully subscribed, the IPO could value the refinery at about $47 billion, potentially adding $23 billion to Dangote’s net worth. Proceeds will fund refinery expansion and a new project in Kenya, aiming to double capacity and reduce Africa’s reliance on imported fuel. The deal underscores a broader shift toward local ownership of strategic energy assets.

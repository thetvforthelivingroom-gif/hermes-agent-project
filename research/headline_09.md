# EXCLUSIVE: Russia hits passenger train on Ukraine's border with Poland, just misses 'diplomatic train' - Reuters

## Summary
A Reuters report details a Russian drone strike on a passenger train near the Ukraine‑Poland border, occurring just after former British Prime Minister Boris Johnson and other European diplomats used the same line. The train, frequently employed by delegations returning from Kyiv, was hit approximately 2 km from the Polish border. No casualties were reported. Ukrainian railway operator Ukrzaliznytsia suggested the ‘diplomatic train’ may have been the intended target. The incident reflects heightened tensions as both sides intensify long‑distance strikes.

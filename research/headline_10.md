# EXCLUSIVE: Indonesia rescuers battle turbulent seas in search for 129 people after passenger ship capsizes - Reuters

## Summary
Reuters reports that a passenger ferry, Virgo Transport 8, capsized in the Java Sea on September 13, 2026, while en route from Surabaya (East Java) to Banjarmasin (South Kalimantan). The vessel was carrying over 240 passengers and crew. Indonesian authorities deployed more than 650 personnel, including navy ships, coast‑guard vessels, helicopters and specialized underwater drones, to conduct search‑and‑rescue operations under rough sea conditions with waves up to three metres.

Initial response rescued six bodies and 108 survivors. Survivors described the vessel listing rapidly, a lack of alarms, and chaotic evacuation. Rescue teams faced difficulties boarding the partially submerged hull, and divers worked to free trapped passengers. National Search and Rescue Agency chief Mohammad Syafii emphasized the ongoing effort, stating that 129 individuals remained missing and that the operation could extend for several more days.

The incident highlights Indonesia’s ongoing challenges with maritime safety in a region prone to severe weather and over‑crowded vessels. Government officials have pledged investigations into possible overload, adherence to safety protocols, and the condition of life‑saving equipment on board. International observers note that similar capsizing events in Southeast Asia have driven recent regulatory reforms aimed at tighter vessel certification and mandatory emergency drills.

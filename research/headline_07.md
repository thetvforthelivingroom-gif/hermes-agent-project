# EXCLUSIVE: World Cup final tug-of-war escalates as Morocco ... Spain stakes claim - Reuters

## Summary
The race to host the 2030 FIFA World Cup final has intensified into a diplomatic showdown. Morocco’s prime minister, Aziz Akhannouch, publicly rebuked the Moroccan Football Federation’s (RMFF) declaration that Casablanca would be the venue, warning that such promises undermine negotiations with co‑hosts Spain and Portugal. He emphasized the need for “respect among brothers” and argued that unilateral claims could jeopardise the broader partnership.

Spain’s football federation president, Rafael Louzan, countered that as the reigning world champions, Spain should naturally host the final. He highlighted Spain’s proven organisational capacity, recent successful delivery of major tournaments, and the logistical advantages of existing stadium infrastructure. Louzan dismissed the Moroccan claim as “political posturing” and insisted that FIFA’s decision should reflect merit and readiness, not electoral promises.

FIFA has not yet announced which of the three co‑hosts—Spain, Portugal or Morocco—will stage the 2030 final. The organization’s spokesperson reiterated that the decision will be made after a thorough assessment of stadium standards, transport links, security arrangements, and legacy benefits. Both parties have begun lobbying FIFA councils, presenting technical dossiers and economic impact studies.

The dispute underscores the high stakes attached to hosting the final: the event is projected to generate upwards of $1 billion in direct tourism revenue for the host city, massive global media exposure, and a lasting boost to sporting infrastructure. For Morocco, securing the final would cement its growing reputation as a football hub following the successful delivery of the 2026 World Cup matches. For Spain, retaining the final would reinforce its status as a premier European sporting destination.

Analysts predict a close vote, noting that while Spain’s facilities are ready, Morocco’s ambitious stadium projects could offer a fresh market and strategic geographic diversity for FIFA. The outcome will likely hinge on FIFA’s evaluation of guarantees, financial assurances, and the ability to meet the stringent technical criteria set out in the 2025 FIFA regulations.

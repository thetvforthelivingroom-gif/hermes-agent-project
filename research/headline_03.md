# EXCLUSIVE: Half of Russia's top diesel-producing refineries cut back output after drone strikes - Reuters

## Summary
In September 2026, drone attacks forced half of Russia’s six leading diesel‑producing refineries to sharply reduce or halt production. The plants – Omsk, Kirishi, Taneco, Volgograd, NORSI and Perm – together account for roughly half of the country’s diesel output. Kirishi was fully shut, while Volgograd and NORSI operated at about ¼ capacity; the others ran at reduced rates. Reuters calculations based on market data suggest the attacks cut diesel output by an estimated 30‑40 percent, pushing global diesel prices higher and prompting Moscow to restrict fuel exports.

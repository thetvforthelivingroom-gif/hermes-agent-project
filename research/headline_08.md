# EXCLUSIVE: Senior Sunni cleric shot dead by unidentified gunmen in southeast Iran - Reuters

## Summary
Reuters reports that Molavi Yusef Gorgij, a senior Sunni Muslim cleric, was killed by unidentified gunmen in southeastern Iran. The incident was confirmed by Iran’s state news agency IR NA. No group has claimed responsibility, and authorities have not provided details on motives or perpetrators. The killing adds to a series of recent violent incidents in the region, highlighting ongoing security tensions.

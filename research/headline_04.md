# EXCLUSIVE: Kennedy Center on brink of bankruptcy, could close as early as Tuesday, Washington Post reports - Reuters

## Summary
Reuters reports that the Kennedy Center faces a severe financial crisis and could be forced to close as early as Tuesday. The board, appointed by President Trump, alleges a $23 million deficit and escalating maintenance costs. Internal documents suggest a two‑year shutdown for extensive renovations may be considered, but the center’s leadership argues that immediate closure is necessary to avoid “certain fiscal collapse.” The situation has sparked political debate, with critics accusing the Trump‑appointed board of using the crisis to justify a rebranding effort.

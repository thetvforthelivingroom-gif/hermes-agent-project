# EXCLUSIVE: Trump approval up from record low, but outlook sours for Republicans, Reuters/Ipsos poll finds - Reuters

## Summary
A Reuters/Ipsos poll shows President Donald Trump’s approval rating rose modestly to 35 % from 33 % but still remains near a record low. The same survey reveals a growing Democratic advantage: 44 % of respondents favor Democrats in a generic congressional ballot versus 37 % for Republicans, the widest gap in a decade. 63 % of Americans disapproved of Trump’s proposal to grant a $5,000 dividend to all citizens if Republicans retain Congress control. The poll, conducted after the Republican midterm convention, reflects shifting public sentiment amid the Iran war’s economic fallout.
